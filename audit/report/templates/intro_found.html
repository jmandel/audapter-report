The core works: formant shifts deliver what they log, and pitch shifts land within a few cents of what was asked.

No blab experiment we inspected is exposed to trial-to-trial carry-over. We checked the OST files and the way conditions are switched between trials in 10 public blab experiments and in blab's unpublished experiment code (including timeWrap, pitchAdaptPD and pitchComp). None uses the settings that let one trial's state leak into the next, and in simulated mixed sessions with the lab's settings, catch trials received no perturbation whatever came before them (details).

What does touch current experiments:

  • Masking noise drops out. Since b2.4 (April 2026) the bundled babble goes silent for [[I01_gap_ms_min:.0f]] ms about every 10 s of running time: in about 1 in [[I01_one_in:.1f]] trials of a masking phase. Data collected before April 2026 are not affected (I-01).
  • The phase vocoder changes loudness. With pitch shifting switched on, the voice is played louder, by up to [[PT5_cue_db:.0f]] dB, unless a time-warp section is loaded. blab's timeWrap design avoids this by keeping a zero-length warp row on its control trials (PT-5).
  • vsaSentence depends on an undocumented blab change. The field perturbation switches on again at every vowel; on the original Audapter 2.1.5 or another fork, only the first vowel of each sentence would be perturbed (F6).
  • Three questions about the public experiment scripts. As committed, vsaCentralize's runner looks up the lab's field on the wrong grid, so most vowels are pushed away from the vowel-space centre instead of toward it (LAB-1); vsaGeneralize's runner perturbs its baseline trials and never scales the field (LAB-2) and plays its fb 4 masking noise [[LAB3_lv_diff:.0f]] dB louder than set (LAB-3). The runners that collected the data may differ; each card says how to check the lab's own data.

Pitfalls for future designs. Carry-over is real with other settings: an offset rule placed after a timer or after the RMS-floor rule fires later on every trial (OST-F1); a PCF left loaded overrides a later field experiment (COORD-1); pitch catch trials made by clearing the PCF can be shifted (OST-F5). Each card gives the safe pattern. Other pitfalls include wrongly logged pitch for low voices with time-domain shifting, crashes or hangs when settings change mid-session, and clicks when resetting during speech (all).

Robustness. A few memory errors need a malformed file or array to show up (all).

None of these produce a warning. Most cards include a fix you can try on this page.
