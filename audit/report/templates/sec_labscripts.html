These are not Audapter bugs. Reading the lab's public experiment scripts, we noticed three settings that look different from what the design describes, so we ran each script's exact parameter sequence through Audapter (the lab's own calc_pertField, synthetic vowels at the lab's default corner-vowel means) to see what a participant would hear. We offer them as questions. The runner that collected the data may differ from the committed one (vsaCentralize's experiment script calls a runner that is not in the repository), and the lab's logged data can settle each question directly: in field mode Audapter logs the shifted formants (sfmts) next to the produced ones (fmts) on every frame.

Audapter accepts all of these silently. Three warnings would have caught them: a field grid outside F1Min..F1Max, bShift2D = 1 with an all-zero two-dimensional field, and fields of p that AudapterIO does not forward.
